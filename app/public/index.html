      WhatsApp & groups

      The phone number WA Box sends from, and which of its groups your team can pick.

      Connection

      1. Open WhatsApp on the phone you'll send from.
      2. Go to Settings, then Linked devices, then Link a device.
      3. Point the phone at this code. It refreshes on its own.

      Open WhatsApp on that phone at least once a week. A linked device logs out if the phone stays offline for about 14 days.

      Safety & settings

      Limits that keep the number looking like a person, not a bot. These apply to everyone.

      Sending

      Always on

      • One message at a time, never in parallel.
      • At least 30 seconds between any two sends.
      • Start times move by up to a minute, at random.
      • "Typing…" shows for a few seconds before each message.
      • Each group gets a message once per send, even after a restart.
      • Nothing sends while WhatsApp is disconnected.
