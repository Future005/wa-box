      Safety & settings

      Limits that keep the number looking like a person, not a bot. These apply to everyone.

      Sending

      Alerts when nobody is watching

      WA Box tells you if WhatsApp disconnects, sending pauses itself, a send is unclear, or a message is missed. Each problem is announced at most once an hour.

      Always on

      • One message at a time, never in parallel.
      • At least 30 seconds between any two sends.
      • Start times move by up to a minute, at random.
      • "Typing…" shows for a few seconds before each message.
      • Each group gets a message once per send, even after a restart.
      • Nothing sends while WhatsApp is disconnected.
      • If a reply from WhatsApp is lost, the group's chat is checked before anything is resent.
      • Only one copy of WA Box can send at a time, even during an update.
      • Three failures in a row pause sending and alert an admin.
      • Weekly messages never send twice in a day and skip missed weeks instead of catching up.
