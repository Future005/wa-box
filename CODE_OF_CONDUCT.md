# Code of conduct

WA Box is built for people who look after communities. Treat each other the way a good community host would.

## Expected

- Be kind and patient, especially with people who are new to self-hosting or to code.
- Assume good intent; ask before you judge.
- Give feedback on the work, not the person. Be specific.
- Credit other people's ideas and work.

## Not okay

- Harassment, insults, or discrimination of any kind.
- Sexual language or imagery, or unwanted attention.
- Publishing someone's private information (phone numbers, chats, addresses) without permission.
- Using WA Box, or discussions about it, to plan spam or messages to people who didn't ask for them.

## If something happens

Report it privately to the maintainer through the contact on [Bhavishya's LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/bhavishyabharadwaj/),
or with **Security → Report a vulnerability** if it's urgent. Reports are kept confidential.
The maintainer may remove comments, close issues, or block people who break these rules.

This code is inspired by the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/), version 2.1.
